How CareCircle Works

1

Create the Medication Plan

Input daily prescriptions, reminder times, dosages, and clinician instructions. Use our smart prescription scanner to parse details quickly.

2

Connect the Care Circle

Share a secure invitation passcode with family and caregivers. Assign tailored permissions: Editor for medical aides, Viewer for family.

3

Stay Protected & In Sync

Track daily adherence streaks, receive timely alerts for upcoming doses, coordinate clinic visits, and generate clinical care handover reports.

Safety & Privacy First

Healthcare-Grade Security Architecture

Senior healthcare records require privacy and reliability. CareCircle enforces granular server-side authorization, cryptographic password hashing, session isolation, CSRF protection, and audit logs.

🔒 Passcode-Based Access
Circle invitations expire automatically and require authorized acceptance.
🛡️ Granular Permissions
Strict server-side role scoping ensures viewers cannot edit prescriptions.
📑 Tamper-Evident Audit
All dose logs, record modifications, and task updates track the author.
