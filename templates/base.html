CareCircle — Intelligent Elderly Care & Medication Management Platform

CareCircle is a care coordination prototype created for the PromptWars × Catalyst hackathon. It organizes clinician-provided medication plans and schedules and facilitates family care coordination. It is not a certified medical device, does not prescribe treatments, and does not alter medical advice.
